Kleos Studio

Privacy policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

Kleos Studio is a program that an agency installs and runs on its own computer to optimize the YouTube channels that it manages. This policy explains which data Kleos Studio gets when a channel owner connects a YouTube channel with the Google sign-in, how the program uses that data, and how the owner can remove it.

Kleos Studio uses YouTube API Services. By connecting a channel, you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's processing of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Who is responsible

Each copy of Kleos Studio is run by the agency that invited you. That agency decides how it uses the program and is responsible for the data on its computer. The publisher of Kleos Studio does not run a server that receives your channel data.

Data that Kleos Studio gets

  • Channel identity: the channel ID and the channel name.
  • Access tokens: the tokens that Google gives after you allow access. Kleos Studio stores them encrypted on the agency's computer.
  • Video information: titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails, links, duration, visibility (public, unlisted, private, scheduled) and public statistics of the videos of the channel.
  • YouTube Analytics reports: views, watch time, average view duration, impressions, click-through rate, likes, comments, shares and subscriber changes per video and per day.

Kleos Studio does not get your Google password, your e-mail inbox, your contacts or your payment data.

How Kleos Studio uses the data

  • It scores the titles, thumbnails, descriptions, tags and links of your videos and shows the reasons.
  • It prepares better versions, which the agency reviews before anything changes on YouTube.
  • After the agency approves a change, it updates the title, description, tags or thumbnail of that video, or changes its visibility (for example from unlisted to public, or at a scheduled time).
  • It shows the real performance of your videos so that the agency can measure the result of each change.

Kleos Studio never deletes videos, comments or captions, and it does not post comments or rate videos. Google shows a wide permission text ("See, edit, and permanently delete ...") because one YouTube permission covers all editing; Kleos Studio uses it only for the changes listed above.

AI services

To write suggestions, Kleos Studio can send the title, description, tags and thumbnail of a video (and for Shorts, the transcript and some frames of the video) to the AI services that the agency selects, for example Anthropic Claude, OpenAI, Google Gemini or Groq. It sends only what a suggestion needs. It does not send your YouTube Analytics reports or your access tokens to AI services.

Sharing

Kleos Studio does not sell your data, does not use it for advertising and does not share it with other parties, except the AI services above and Google itself (to read and update your channel). Kleos Studio's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage and deletion

  • All data stays on the agency's computer. Tokens are stored encrypted.
  • Kleos Studio refreshes the stored YouTube data regularly while the channel is connected.
  • When the agency disconnects your channel, Kleos Studio deletes the stored tokens of the channel.
  • Ask the agency to delete all stored data of your channel at any time. The agency deletes it from Kleos Studio.

Remove access

Remove the access of Kleos Studio at any time on the Google account permissions page (the app shows there with the name that the agency gave it). After that, Kleos Studio can no longer read or change your channel.

Contact

For questions about your data, contact the agency that invited you, or write to the support e-mail address that Google shows on the sign-in screen of the app.

Περίληψη στα ελληνικά

Το Kleos Studio τρέχει στον υπολογιστή του γραφείου που σε προσκάλεσε. Παίρνει το όνομα και το ID του καναλιού, τα στοιχεία των βίντεο και τα στατιστικά YouTube Analytics. Τα κλειδιά πρόσβασης αποθηκεύονται κρυπτογραφημένα. Αλλαγές στο YouTube γίνονται μόνο μετά από έγκριση του γραφείου. Δεν διαγράφει ποτέ βίντεο, σχόλια ή υπότιτλους και δεν πουλάει ούτε μοιράζεται δεδομένα (εκτός από τις υπηρεσίες AI για τις προτάσεις, χωρίς τα στατιστικά Analytics). Ανακαλείς την πρόσβαση οποιαδήποτε στιγμή στη σελίδα αδειών του λογαριασμού Google.
