ViewForge

Privacy policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

ViewForge is a program that an agency installs and runs on its own computer to optimize the YouTube channels that it manages. This policy explains which data ViewForge gets when a channel owner connects a YouTube channel with the Google sign-in, how the program uses that data, and how the owner can remove it.

ViewForge uses YouTube API Services. By connecting a channel, you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's processing of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Who is responsible

Each copy of ViewForge is run by the agency that invited you. That agency decides how it uses the program and is responsible for the data on its computer. The publisher of ViewForge does not run a server that receives your channel data. If the agency gives you a private link, an encrypted copy of your page is stored online under the agency's own Cloudflare account (see "Storage and deletion").

Data that ViewForge gets

  • Channel identity: the channel ID and the channel name.
  • Access tokens: the tokens that Google gives after you allow access. ViewForge stores them encrypted on the agency's computer.
  • Video information: titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails, links, duration, visibility (public, unlisted, private, scheduled) and public statistics of the videos of the channel.
  • YouTube Analytics reports: views, watch time, average view duration, audience retention, impressions, click-through rate, likes, comments, shares and subscriber changes per video and per day, and the age, gender, country, traffic sources and devices of the viewers of the channel (only as totals, never as single viewers).
  • Captions: when the agency asks for a retention review of a video, ViewForge reads the captions of that video.

ViewForge does not get your Google password, your e-mail inbox, your contacts or your payment data.

How ViewForge uses the data

  • It scores the titles, thumbnails, descriptions, tags and links of your videos and shows the reasons.
  • It prepares better versions, which the agency reviews before anything changes on YouTube.
  • After the agency approves a change, it updates the title, description, tags or thumbnail of that video, or changes its visibility (for example from unlisted to public, or at a scheduled time).
  • It shows the real performance of your videos so that the agency can measure the result of each change, and it makes a monthly results report for you.
  • It shows where the viewers of a video leave, so that the agency can advise you about the next video.
  • It makes a media kit with the numbers and the audience of your channel, for you to give to sponsors.
  • It makes a page for you, for a phone or a PC, with the numbers of your channel, the alerts and the changes. The agency can give you this page as a file or as a private link.

ViewForge never deletes videos, comments or captions, and it does not post comments or rate videos. Google shows a wide permission text ("See, edit, and permanently delete ...") because one YouTube permission covers all editing; ViewForge uses it only for the changes listed above.

AI services

To write suggestions, ViewForge can send the title, description, tags and thumbnail of a video (and for Shorts, the transcript and some frames of the video) to the AI services that the agency selects, for example Anthropic Claude, OpenAI, Google Gemini or Groq. It sends only what a suggestion needs.

The one exception for YouTube Analytics data is the retention review. Only when the agency asks for this review of one video, ViewForge sends the retention facts of that video (the moments where the audience changes, and how much) and the words spoken at those moments. It does not send other YouTube Analytics reports, and it never sends your access tokens to AI services. Ask the agency not to use this review for your channel if you do not want it.

The second exception is a sponsor pitch, and only after you agree with the agency. ViewForge then sends the numbers of your channel (views, the latest videos, audience, retention) and what the agency wrote about the brand to the AI service. The agency can then send your numbers to that brand. Withdraw your OK at any time.

Sharing

ViewForge does not sell your data, does not use it for advertising and does not share it with other parties, except the AI services above and Google itself (to read and update your channel). Cloudflare stores the encrypted copy of your page if you have a private link, and it cannot read that copy. The results report, the page for you and the media kit are files on the agency's computer. The agency gives them to you, and it gives a media kit or a sponsor pitch to a sponsor only with your OK. ViewForge's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage and deletion

  • All data stays on the agency's computer, except the private link below. Tokens are stored encrypted and never leave the agency's computer.
  • Private link: if the agency gives you one, an encrypted copy of your page is stored with Cloudflare, under the agency's account. The key is only in your link, in the part that browsers do not send, so Cloudflare cannot read the page. Anyone who has the link can read the page, so keep it private. The copy deletes itself after 7 days without an update. It is also deleted when the agency cancels the link or disconnects your channel, and when your Google access ends.
  • ViewForge refreshes the stored YouTube data regularly while the channel is connected. It deletes a retention review after 30 days.
  • When the agency disconnects your channel, ViewForge deletes the stored tokens of the channel.
  • Ask the agency to delete all stored data of your channel at any time. The agency deletes it from ViewForge.

Remove access

Remove the access of ViewForge at any time on the Google account permissions page (the app shows there with the name that the agency gave it). After that, ViewForge can no longer read or change your channel.

Contact

For questions about your data, contact the agency that invited you, or write to the support e-mail address that Google shows on the sign-in screen of the app.

Περίληψη στα ελληνικά

Το ViewForge τρέχει στον υπολογιστή του γραφείου που σε προσκάλεσε. Παίρνει το όνομα και το ID του καναλιού, τα στοιχεία των βίντεο και τα στατιστικά YouTube Analytics. Τα κλειδιά πρόσβασης αποθηκεύονται κρυπτογραφημένα. Αλλαγές στο YouTube γίνονται μόνο μετά από έγκριση του γραφείου. Δεν διαγράφει ποτέ βίντεο, σχόλια ή υπότιτλους και δεν πουλάει ούτε μοιράζεται δεδομένα. Εξαίρεση είναι οι υπηρεσίες AI για τις προτάσεις: δεν παίρνουν στατιστικά Analytics, εκτός από την ανάλυση διατήρησης κοινού ενός βίντεο, αν τη ζητήσει το γραφείο (τις στιγμές που φεύγουν οι θεατές και τα λόγια εκείνη την ώρα). Το media kit και η πρόταση σε χορηγό δίνονται μόνο με τη δική σου έγκριση, και τότε τα νούμερα του καναλιού περνούν και από την υπηρεσία AI. Αν το γραφείο σου δώσει προσωπικό σύνδεσμο, ένα κρυπτογραφημένο αντίγραφο της σελίδας σου μένει στο Cloudflare. Το κλειδί είναι μόνο στον σύνδεσμο, άρα το Cloudflare δεν μπορεί να τη διαβάσει. Το αντίγραφο σβήνει μόνο του μετά από 7 ημέρες χωρίς ενημέρωση, καθώς και όταν ακυρωθεί ο σύνδεσμος ή αποσυνδεθεί το κανάλι. Ανακαλείς την πρόσβαση οποιαδήποτε στιγμή στη σελίδα αδειών του λογαριασμού Google.
