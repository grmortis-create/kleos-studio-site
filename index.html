Kleos Studio

A YouTube optimization studio for creator agencies

Kleos Studio is a program that an agency installs on its own computer. It audits the videos of the YouTube channels that the agency manages and helps to make them better: titles, thumbnails, descriptions, tags, links and Shorts.

What Kleos Studio does

  • It scores each title, thumbnail, description, tag set and link from 1 to 10, with clear reasons.
  • It writes better versions with AI, which the agency reviews and approves.
  • It publishes the approved changes to YouTube and can make an unlisted video public at a selected time.
  • It reads the real performance of the channel (views, watch time, impressions, click-through rate) to measure the result of each change.

Connecting a YouTube channel

An agency connects a channel only with the permission of the channel owner, through the official Google sign-in. The owner can remove this permission at any time on the Google account permissions page. Kleos Studio never deletes videos, comments or captions.

Read how Kleos Studio handles your data in the privacy policy.

Στα ελληνικά

Το Kleos Studio είναι πρόγραμμα που εγκαθιστά ένα γραφείο (agency) στον δικό του υπολογιστή. Βαθμολογεί και βελτιώνει τίτλους, μικρογραφίες, περιγραφές, tags, links και Shorts των καναλιών YouTube που διαχειρίζεται. Ένα κανάλι συνδέεται μόνο με την άδεια του κατόχου του, μέσω της επίσημης σύνδεσης Google, και η άδεια ανακαλείται οποιαδήποτε στιγμή. Το Kleos Studio δεν διαγράφει ποτέ βίντεο, σχόλια ή υπότιτλους.
